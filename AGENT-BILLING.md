# Render Check for agent workflows

Render Check is called after an image or video has been generated and before another paid attempt. The agent supplies the exported media and brief, interprets the returned evidence, and asks the user before paying for another render. Results can require manual review; they do not guarantee a future render will work.

## Account, balance, and checkout

A customer can create a review key at https://voopa-render-check.onrender.com/plans. They can connect the same key to an MCP-capable host using the Bearer token at `https://voopa-render-check.onrender.com/mcp/usage`. Treat the key like a password: never put it in public source code, logs, or chat transcripts. Customers may link Google from the existing account to recover access; existing key holders must connect that key before linking Google, or a separate account will be created.

Signing in or linking a verified Google account grants a one-time trial of 25 units. This covers up to five video checks of five seconds each, or 25 image checks; longer video checks use more units. A new browser or a new review key does not refresh the trial. Separate Google accounts can each receive a trial. The anonymous `/mcp` preview stays available without login, but shares a global rate limit and may be temporarily unavailable. To use a personal trial balance, connect the review key to `/mcp/usage`; the legacy `/mcp/paid` route cannot run unmetered media checks. Existing balances and keys continue to work on `/mcp/usage`.

Use `get_usage_balance` to check units. `estimate_review_units` estimates a review. The server verifies the actual video duration before debiting. An image review costs 1 unit; a video review costs 5 units per started 5 seconds. Call `review_metered_image` or `review_metered_video` with a unique `operationId`, then `decide_metered_retry` with the same ID after inspecting the returned evidence. The retry decision is not charged again. `get_review_operation_status` checks a previously submitted operation; `recover_interrupted_review` can release an unfinished reservation after ten minutes.

Plans are $5 USD per month for 250 units, or one-time packs of $5 for 250 units, $15 for 750, and $30 for 1,500. Packs do not renew. Stripe handles payments; the user must authorize and complete checkout. The agent must not enter card details or assume permission to spend. A checkout session can be created without completing a purchase. Redirect the user to the Stripe URL returned by the service, and check the balance again after Stripe confirms payment. Do not assume payment or credits were successful merely because a checkout page opened.

For an app integrating programmatically, `POST /billing/account` with an empty JSON object creates an account and returns its private key. Pass that key as `Authorization: Bearer ...` to `GET /billing/usage-balance`. `GET /billing/packs` lists the current pack IDs and amounts; `POST /billing/checkout` accepts a unique UUID `attempt`, one of the returned `packId` values, and `returnTo: "plans"`, and returns the Stripe checkout URL. Keep the same attempt ID when retrying the same checkout request. Subscription checkout is available through `POST /billing/subscription-checkout` with a UUID attempt. Store credentials in the host's secret manager. Never publish a key or checkout URL.

For sound-level review, `inspect_metered_audio_levels` checks one video interval of 1–30 seconds. It costs 5 units per started 5 seconds of the selected interval within the verified video duration; supply a unique `operationId`. A failed inspection releases its reservation. Compare two clips with separate calls and separate charges. Measurements are listening cues, not voice identity, perceptual loudness, clipping or lip-sync verdicts.

`check_metered_black_gaps` and `check_metered_freezes` inspect a selected 1–120 second interval, and `scan_metered_repeated_shots` inspects a selected 4–60 second interval. Each requires `renderVideoUrl`, `durationSeconds` and a unique `operationId`; `fromSeconds` defaults to zero. Each costs 5 units per started 5 seconds of the selected interval within the verified video duration. Retrying the same operation ID does not debit twice, and a failed inspection releases reserved units. Results are candidate ranges for review: dark scenes, pauses and repeated shots can be intentional, and the algorithms can miss problems. Inspect any unscanned parts separately. Separate checks are charged separately.

The free preview at `/mcp` remains rate-limited and distinct from the usage-based endpoint. The original `/mcp/paid` endpoint remains available to customers with legacy credits; 1 original credit corresponds to 5 usage units. Calling the usage-based endpoint does not itself make a new generation or approve spending.

`compare_metered_continuity` takes two public video URLs, two frame timestamps, a focus and a unique `operationId`. It costs 5 units for the comparison. The returned frames are evidence for a vision-capable agent, not an automatic count or continuity verdict. Occlusion, viewpoint and unsampled moments can make the outcome uncertain. Reusing the same operation ID does not charge again; failures release the reservation.

`compare_metered_reference_audio` takes a video URL, reference audio URL, duration (2–15 seconds), optional start times and a unique `operationId`. It charges 5 units per started 5 seconds within both verified audio streams and video duration. Failed or truncated checks release the reservation; reuse of an operation ID cannot charge twice. It compares waveform similarity in one-second and quarter-second windows, not voice identity, lyrics, source correctness or lip sync. Quiet windows are inconclusive and short windows are sensitive to mixing. Low similarity may be caused by editing or mixing; periodic audio can yield ambiguous alignment.

`compare_shot_inventory` is a free, local calculation on the supplied approved shot IDs and delivered inventory. It charges no units, calls no model and does not inspect media. The caller must list items in timeline order and state whether the inventory covers the full edit. Missing IDs, repeats, order differences and optional single-track gaps or overlaps are leads for review, not proof of a missing shot or an error.

`measure_metered_region_motion` measures a caller-selected rectangle for 1–30 seconds and costs 5 units per started five seconds of the verified interval. It reports sampled pixel-change evidence only; a human or agent must inspect the footage, and camera movement can mislead. Failed checks release reserved units and repeating an operation ID does not charge twice.

`compare_metered_shot_ranges` compares two caller-selected non-overlapping ranges of 2–8 seconds each. It costs 5 units per started five seconds of the combined range length. A repeated operation ID is not charged twice and failed work releases reserved units. This does not search the full edit; repeated or similar scenes may be intentional and transformed repeats may be missed.

`measure_metered_loudness` measures EBU R128 integrated loudness and estimated true peak in 3–30 seconds of a video. It costs 5 units per started five seconds of the verified shared video/audio interval. It is evidence only, not a clipping, platform compliance, sound-quality, voice-identity or lip-sync verdict. Quiet material may be inconclusive. Failed checks release units; repeating an operation ID does not charge twice.

`compare_lyrics_transcript` is a free text-only comparison of an approved lyric excerpt and a user- or agent-supplied transcript of the same excerpt. It does not transcribe or hear audio; listen to suggested differences because transcription can mishear singing. It does not assess singing technique or lip synchronization.

`scan_metered_cross_window_repeats` searches two chronological, non-overlapping selected windows of 4–30 seconds each. It costs 5 units per started five seconds of their combined duration. Candidates require review, do not establish intent, and may miss altered or out-of-window repeats. Reusing the same operation ID does not charge again; failed work releases reserved units.

`check_metered_expected_audio` checks one selected 1–30 second interval for a missing audio track or sustained quiet. The cost is 5 units per started 5 seconds of verified video. Reusing the operation ID does not incur a second charge. Quiet may be intentional; other audio can mask a missing effect. This is not sound-event recognition or audiovisual sync verification.

`check_metered_dark_side_bars` costs 5 units per started 5 seconds of the verified selected 1–30 second interval. It examines one frame per second and flags dark side edges with a brighter center. Repeated operation IDs do not charge twice. Intentional dark borders can trigger it; colored or blurred bars, framing problems and unsampled frames may be missed.
